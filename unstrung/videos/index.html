unstrung Videos

Unstrung is an Electron-based application designed for blind guitar players. Its focus is making inaccessible learning and practice materials accessible. It uses semantic HTML to generate accessible and usable HTML documents based, initially at least, on guitar pro and musicXML files.

Unstrung also has many features to assist with practice including playing along with chord progressions. It is evolving rapidly (as of Fall, 2026).

All of these are on the eyesunstrung YouTube channel.

unstrung App - chord practice (play along)

Unstrung has a chord practice tool. You select a key, timing, tempo, and a few other properties. Unstrung generates a sequence of chords, in your selected key with possibly some nearby chords. You can play the sequence in a loop for a chosen number of times, or until your fingers are raw. You can opt to have the chord for the next measure announced on the last beat of the current measure. It's designed to help nail down chord transitions and learn the different chords in a key.
